# Publication and research context

## Source publication

This is the visualization accompanying [*Towards digital twinning for multi-domain simulation workflows in urban design: a case study in Gothenburg*](https://research.chalmers.se/publication/546286), Journal of Building Performance Simulation **18**(3), 311–332 (2025), DOI [10.1080/19401493.2024.2320112](https://doi.org/10.1080/19401493.2024.2320112). Footnote 4 of the [published article](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/19401493.2024.2320112) links to the original `ComputationSustainableDesign/multidomainvis/` deployment.

The research authors are Alex Arnoldo Gonzalez Caceres, Franziska Hunger, Jens Forssén, Sanjay Somanath, Andreas Mark, Vasilis Naserentin, Joakim Bohlin, Anders Logg, Beata Stahre Wästberg, Dominika Komisarczyk, Fredrik Edelvik and Alexander Hollberg. Software credits remain separate in the [repository overview](../README.md) and [citation file](../CITATION.cff).

## Connection to decarbonAIte

[decarbonAIte](https://research.chalmers.se/project/10568) is related research on digital twins and renovation decision support. Alex Gonzalez Caceres and Alexander Hollberg participate in both research contexts.

A documented bridge is [the SI3FP façade-parser publication](https://research.chalmers.se/publication/547956): its Chalmers record associates both decarbonAIte (**2021-02759**) and DTCC (**2019-00041**). The connection recorded here is shared researchers and DTCC-related work. The viewer paper does not identify decarbonAIte's grant in its funding statement.

The [decarbonAIte software repository](https://github.com/SB-Chalmers/decarbonAIte) contains the related renovation LCA/LCC workflow. Its project-specific people, funding and dates are documented there.

Metadata and source links checked on 2026-10-22. No changes to the viewer's scientific methods or datasets accompany this documentation update.
